# 12. Can you guarantee a number of meetings?

**They say:** "Can you guarantee me a number of meetings each month?"

## What they really mean

They have been let down before, or they cannot afford for this to fail. They want you to carry some of the risk.

## Say it out loud

No, and I'd be careful about promising you that without knowing how your market will respond. Whether people book depends on your offer, your price and the timing, and some of that I can't control. What I can promise is the work. I'll tell you exactly what counts as a booked call, which is {{booked_call}}, you'll see the numbers {{report_rhythm}}, and the terms are {{contract_terms}}, so you know what you're committing to from the start.

## Send it by email

Subject: About promising a number of meetings

Hi,

I will not promise a number, because results depend on your offer, your price and the timing, and I do not control all of that.

Here is what I will commit to. A booked call means {{booked_call}}. You will see the numbers {{report_rhythm}}. The terms are {{contract_terms}}.

If that is not enough certainty right now, I understand, and I would rather you know that before we start.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

What would have to be true after the first month for you to feel this was working?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they only want to pay when a specific thing happens, usage pricing fits that better. HubSpot's agent charges per lead recommended for outreach, for example, though that counts a recommended lead and not a meeting. Point out the difference.
