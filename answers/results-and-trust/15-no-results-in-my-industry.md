## When the tool is the better fit

If they must see published case studies from their exact field before they can buy, a vendor or specialist with that public proof is the better fit for now. Tell them, then ask if you can come back once you have it.

None about any vendor. Your proof line must be true and something you can show. If you have none yet, leave it empty and the kit uses an honest line for you.
