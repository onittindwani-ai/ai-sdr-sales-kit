# 11. You are tiny compared to Salesforce, so why trust you?

**They say:** "Salesforce is a giant. You're a small shop. Why should I trust you with this?"

## What they really mean

They are worried you will disappear, drop the ball, or be learning at their expense.

## Say it out loud

You're right, I'm small and Salesforce isn't, so please don't pick me for my size. What small gets you is this. You'd deal with me directly, I focus on {{niche}}, and if something goes wrong you know exactly who to call. {{proof_line}} And you're not being asked to trust me blindly, because {{approval_step}}, and the terms are {{contract_terms}}.

## Ask this next

What would you need to see in the first few weeks to feel good about this?

## When the tool is the better fit

If their company needs a large vendor to pass a formal security review, a procurement process or a global rollout, a big platform is the better fit. A small agency cannot honestly stand in for that, so say it.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. Your proof line must be true and something you can show.
