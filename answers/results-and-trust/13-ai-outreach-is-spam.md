# 13. AI outreach is spam and it will hurt my brand

**They say:** "I get those AI emails all the time. I don't want my name on that."

## What they really mean

They care about their reputation and they are picturing the worst messages they have seen.

## Say it out loud

I feel the same way about a lot of what lands in my inbox, so let's make sure yours isn't that. The fix is sending less and aiming better. The list is only {{their_customers}} who could really buy from you, and each message has a real reason for writing to that person. Nothing goes out in your name that you haven't seen, because {{approval_step}}. And anyone who says stop gets stopped, straight away.

## Send it by email

Subject: Keeping your name off the spam pile

Hi,

I agree with you. A lot of that outreach is hard to read, and I would not want it under my name either.

We send fewer messages and aim them better: only {{their_customers}} who could really buy from you, each with a real reason for writing. Before anything goes out, {{approval_step}}. Anyone who asks to stop is stopped at once.

If you read the first batch and do not like it, nothing gets sent.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

What's the last cold message you actually liked, and what made it different?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they want every message written and sent by their own staff from inside their own system, with no outside party involved, an in house tool suits them better. Respect that, because it is their name.

None about any vendor. Only say this if you really keep to it, including stopping anyone who asks.
