# 08. I would rather hire a junior SDR

**They say:** "For that money I could hire a junior sales rep and have someone of my own."

## What they really mean

They want a person who belongs to the company and builds up knowledge there. They may also be wary of paying someone outside.

## Say it out loud

That can be a good call, especially if you want someone in the building and you want to grow a team. The thing to weigh up is that a new hire needs someone to train them, manage them and cover for them if they leave, and right now that someone would be you. ## When the tool is the better fit

If they have a sales manager, want a permanent team, and sell something that needs long personal relationships, hiring a person, perhaps with a tool to help them, is the better fit. Encourage it and offer to hand over what you know.
