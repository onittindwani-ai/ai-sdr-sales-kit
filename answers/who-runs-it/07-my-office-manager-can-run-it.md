# 07. My office manager can run the tool

**They say:** "My office manager is good with computers. She can just run one of these tools."

## What they really mean

They would rather use someone they already pay. They have probably not thought about what would come off that person's desk.

## Say it out loud

They might be great at it, and I'd never assume otherwise. What I'd look at is their week. Outbound only works if someone does it every single week, and it's usually the first thing to get dropped when the day job gets busy. If they've really got protected hours for this and they like it, then a tool and their time could be the right answer. If not, {{agency_name}} takes it off their plate, and you both just get a plain report {{report_rhythm}}.

## Send it by email

Subject: Your office manager running this

Hi,

If they have real time for it and they enjoy it, that could work well, and I would not talk you out of it.

The thing I would check is what gives way when the week gets busy. Outbound needs doing every week to work at all.

If you would rather leave their week as it is, {{agency_name}} can run the whole thing, and all either of you has to read is a plain report {{report_rhythm}}.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

What would they stop doing to make room for this every week?

## When the tool is the better fit

If that person has protected weekly hours, is curious about sales, and someone can coach them on messaging, a tool plus their time is the better and cheaper choice. Say so, and offer to look over their first batch of messages.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. This is your own position, so only say it if it is true of how you work.
