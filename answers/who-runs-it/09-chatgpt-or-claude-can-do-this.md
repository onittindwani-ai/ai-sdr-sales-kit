# 09. ChatGPT or Claude can just do this for me

**They say:** "Can't I just ask ChatGPT or Claude to do my outreach?"

## What they really mean

They use AI already and it seems to do most things. They want to know what you add on top of a subscription they could buy themselves.

## Say it out loud

They can do a lot of it, and I use AI every day, so I won't pretend otherwise. They'll write a good message if you tell them who it's for and what to say. What they don't do by themselves is own the whole thing week after week, so the sending setup, the list, the replies, the follow ups and the judgment about what to change. That running of it is what you'd be paying me for. If you enjoy that kind of work, start with those tools and I'll happily point you the right way.

## When the tool is the better fit

If they only need a small number of careful, personal messages and they are happy to send them by hand, an AI assistant is all they need. Tell them that and do not sell them a service.
