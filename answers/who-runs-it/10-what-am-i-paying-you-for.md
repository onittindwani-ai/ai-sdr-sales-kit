# 10. If you are just using AI, what am I paying you for?

**They say:** "So you're just using AI as well. What exactly am I paying you for?"

## What they really mean

They suspect the service is a thin layer over software, and they want to know someone is really responsible for it.

## Say it out loud

Fair, and I'll be open about it, yes, AI does a lot of the writing and the research here. You're not paying for the AI, though. You're paying for someone to decide who to go after, make sure what goes out is accurate, handle the replies and be the one who answers for it when something isn't working. And before anything starts, {{approval_step}}. So you get the speed of the software, with a person who's responsible for the result.

## When the tool is the better fit

If they want to run it themselves and only need the software, without anyone being answerable for results, then they should buy the software. Paying for a service makes no sense for someone who wants to hold the wheel.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. Only say this if a person on your team really checks the messages and handles the replies.
