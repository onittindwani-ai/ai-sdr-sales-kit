# 23. Tools will replace you soon anyway

**They say:** "Won't all of this be automatic in a year? Why sign up with you now?"

## What they really mean

They do not want to commit to something that may look silly soon, and they are testing whether you are honest about AI.

## Say it out loud

Some of what I do, yes, probably, and I'd be lying if I told you otherwise. The tools keep getting better, and I use them as they do. That's partly why the terms are {{contract_terms}}. As long as someone in your business has to decide who to go after and answer for what's sent, that's where I'm useful. The day a tool does that for you without anybody watching it, I'll be the first to tell you to switch.

## When the tool is the better fit

If a product shows up that truly does this for their kind of business and they can see it working, they should use it. Keep your promise to say so, because your word is what you are selling.
