# 24. Let us wait and see what Hunter does

**They say:** "Salesforce's agent is about to come out. Let's wait and see what it does first."

## What they really mean

They would like a reason not to decide today. Waiting feels safe and costs nothing that they can see.

## Say it out loud

You can, and there's no pressure from me. When I last checked, Salesforce had said it was in pilot, with general availability planned for November 2026. But waiting only makes sense if you'd really use it, and from what's been published, that means being a Salesforce customer with reps to set its goals. If that's not you, nothing changes for you on release day, and you've just waited. If it is you, let's wait and look at it together.
