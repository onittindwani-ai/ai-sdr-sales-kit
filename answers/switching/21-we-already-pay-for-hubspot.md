# 21. We already pay for HubSpot, so why add you?

**They say:** "We're already on HubSpot. Why would I add another cost?"

## What they really mean

They do not want to pay twice, and they may feel they should be getting more from what they already have.

## Say it out loud

Then let's use what you're already paying for. HubSpot lists its prospecting agent on Starter, Professional and Enterprise, so it's worth checking whether you've got it before you spend anything with me. If you have someone who'd work those leads each week, that may be all you need. Where I come in is when nobody has that time. Then {{agency_name}} runs it, and you get {{outcome}} without adding that work to anyone's week.

Subject: Getting more from HubSpot first

Hi,

If someone on your team can work those leads every week, that may be all you need.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

Who on your team logs into HubSpot every day, and what do they do there?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they are already on a plan that lists the agent, and someone has weekly time to act on what it recommends, they should use it. That is simply the better deal, and telling them so builds trust.

## Facts this answer uses

HubSpot's prospecting agent page (source 5), checked 2026-11-09. Pay per lead and credits apply, so do not call it free. Recheck the page on the day you use this.
