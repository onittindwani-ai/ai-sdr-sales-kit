# 22. We will move to Salesforce later anyway

**They say:** "We're planning to go onto Salesforce eventually, so why start with you?"

## What they really mean

They do not want to build something now that they will have to throw away.

## Say it out loud

That's a good plan if that's where you're headed, and nothing I do gets in the way. Think of this as what happens before that. Right now you need {{outcome}}, and you don't yet have the sales team that Salesforce's agent is made to work with. If we do this well, by the time you move, you'll know which people reply, which messages work and what a good lead looks like. And that all comes with you, because {{data_exit}}.

## Ask this next

Roughly when would that move happen, and who would own it?

## When the tool is the better fit

If the move is close, the team is already hired and the Salesforce setup is budgeted, it is better to wait and start there. A few weeks with you would only add noise to that project.

Salesforce's outbound agent page (source 2), checked 2026-11-09. "Made to work with a sales team" is an opinion based on that page saying a sales team sets its rules.
