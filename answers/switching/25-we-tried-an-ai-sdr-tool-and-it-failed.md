# 25. We tried an AI SDR tool and it did not work

**They say:** "We tried one of these AI sales tools already. It was a waste of money."

## What they really mean

They were burned, they feel a little foolish about it, and they do not want to go through it again.

## Say it out loud

I'm sorry, that's a frustrating way to spend money. Before I say anything about what I'd do differently, can I ask what actually happened? Because it can go wrong in different ways, like the wrong people on the list, a message with no real reason to reply, or nobody following up when replies came in. I can't tell you mine would've gone differently until I know which it was. And if the problem was the offer itself, I'll tell you, because outreach can't fix that.

## Send it by email

Subject: What went wrong last time

Hi,

Sorry to hear that. It is a frustrating way to spend money.

Before I tell you what I would do differently, I would like to know what happened. Was it the wrong people, a message with no real reason to reply, or nobody following up on the replies?

Each of those has a different fix, and if the real problem was the offer, I will tell you that too, because outreach cannot fix it.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

What did you see happen, and when did you decide to stop?

## When the tool is the better fit

If the tool only failed because nobody had time to run it, and they now have someone who does, going back to it is the better and cheaper move. Tell them that, and offer to look over the setup.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. This is your own position, so only say it if it is true of how you work.
