# 05. A tool like Hunter has to be cheaper than you

**They say:** "Software like Hunter has to cost less than paying an agency, right?"

## What they really mean

They assume software always beats people on price, and they want you to admit it or prove otherwise.

## Say it out loud

I can't honestly tell you that either way, because when I last looked, Salesforce's page for it didn't show a price, and I'm not going to guess. What I can tell you is that mine costs {{monthly_price}}, the terms are {{contract_terms}}, and I can show you exactly what that covers. If you get a real quote from Salesforce, send it over and we'll compare them line by line, including what you'd need in place to run it, like the account and the people.

## Send it by email

Subject: What software costs compared with me

Hi,

I do not know what it will cost you. When I last checked, Salesforce's page for Hunter did not list a price, so I would rather not guess.

Mine is {{monthly_price}}. The terms are {{contract_terms}}, and it covers everything through to {{booked_call}}.

If you get a quote from Salesforce, send it over and I will put the 2 side by side with you, including the account and the people you would need around it.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

If the software cost you nothing at all, who would run it day to day?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they get a real quote that is lower than your fee, and they already have the Salesforce account and the people to run it, the software is the better deal. Help them see that and part as friends.

## Facts this answer uses

Salesforce's outbound agent page (source 2) showed no price when checked on 2026-11-09. Never state a price for this agent unless Salesforce publishes one and you have read it yourself.
