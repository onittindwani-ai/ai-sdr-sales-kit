# 02. HubSpot's agent is $1 a lead, and you cost far more

**They say:** "HubSpot charges $1 a lead for its agent. Why would I pay you a monthly fee?"

## What they really mean

They are comparing 2 prices side by side, and they have not noticed that the 2 prices buy different things.

## Say it out loud

You're right that those numbers look very different. HubSpot's page says you pay $1 for each lead its agent recommends outreach for, and that's a clear way to price it. But a recommended lead isn't a booked call yet, so someone has to own what happens next, and you'd need a HubSpot plan to run it on. My {{monthly_price}} covers the whole job, all the way through to {{booked_call}}. So the real question is whether you've got someone to do the part after the recommendation.

## Send it by email

Subject: $1 a lead compared with what I charge

Hi,

You are right to compare them. HubSpot says you pay $1 for each lead its agent recommends outreach for, and it is offered on its Starter, Professional and Enterprise plans.

The thing to compare is what you get at the end. There it is a recommended lead. With {{agency_name}}, it is {{booked_call}}, for {{monthly_price}}.

If someone on your side can take those recommended leads and work them every week, HubSpot may well be the cheaper way. If nobody can, that is the gap I fill.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

If that agent handed you a list of recommended leads on Monday, who would work them that week?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they already pay for HubSpot and have a person with real weekly time to work recommended leads, the agent is probably the better and cheaper choice. Tell them to try it first, and leave the door open.
