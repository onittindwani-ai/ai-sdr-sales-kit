# 04. I will just set up an AI tool myself this weekend

**They say:** "I'm pretty handy. I'll set up one of these AI tools myself over a weekend."

## What they really mean

They like being in control, or they do not want to pay until they have tried it. They may be picturing setup as the whole job.

## Say it out loud

You could, and honestly, setting it up is the easy part. What takes the time is everything after that, because someone has to keep the list clean, read the replies, fix the messages that aren't landing and follow up with the people who said not right now. If you've got a few hours for that every week, try it yourself first, I mean that. If you'd rather spend those hours running the business, that's what {{agency_name}} is for.

## Send it by email

Subject: Doing it yourself this weekend

Hi,

Go for it if you enjoy this sort of thing. A weekend is enough to get a tool switched on.

The work I would plan for is what comes after: keeping the list clean, reading replies, changing the messages that are not landing, and following up with people who said not right now. That part repeats every week.

If you find it is taking more of your week than you hoped, I am happy to pick it up from wherever you have got to.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

Once it's set up, how many hours a week could you give it for the next 3 months?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they enjoy the work, have the hours every week, and want to learn outbound for themselves, doing it alone with a tool is the better path. They will learn their own market first hand, and that is worth a lot.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. This is your own position, so only say it if it is true of how you work.
