# 03. Clay and a few tools would be cheaper than you

**They say:** "I could just get Clay and a couple of other tools for less than your fee."

## What they really mean

They have seen people talk about building their own stack. They are counting the software bill and not yet counting who has to build and run it.

## Say it out loud

It might be, on the software bill alone, and I won't argue with that. Clay is made for people who like to build, and that's the point, it's something you build with. Someone still has to pick the targets, build the workflow, write the messages and check what comes out every week. If you've got that person, go for it. If that person would end up being you, the cost you've left out is your own hours, and {{monthly_price}} is what keeps those hours yours.

## Ask this next

If you built this yourself, which hours in your week would you take it from?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they have someone technical who enjoys building workflows and has time to keep them running, Clay and similar tools give more control and can cost less than you. Tell them so, because that buyer would not stay with you anyway.
