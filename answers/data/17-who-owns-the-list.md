# 17. Who owns the list and the data if we stop?

**They say:** "If I stop working with you, do I lose all of it?"

## What they really mean

They have been stuck with a vendor before, or they can see how easily it could happen.

## Say it out loud

No, and it's a good thing to ask anyone you work with. If we stop, {{data_exit}}, and I'll put that in writing before we start. I'd much rather you stay because it's working than because your data is stuck with me. So let's write down what you get, in what format, and how quickly, and then neither of us has to wonder about it later.

## Send it by email

Subject: What you keep if we stop

Hi,

Good question to ask anyone. If we stop working together, {{data_exit}}.

I will write that into the agreement, including the format and how quickly you get it. I would rather you stay because it is working, and never because your data is stuck with me.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

Has a vendor made it hard for you to leave before, and what happened?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they want everything to live in their own accounts from day one, software running under their own login makes ownership simple. Say so, or offer to run the work inside their accounts if you are able to.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. Only promise what your agreement really says.
