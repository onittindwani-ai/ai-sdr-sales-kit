# 18. I have no CRM, so do I need Salesforce or HubSpot first?

**They say:** "We don't even have a CRM. Shouldn't I sort that out with Salesforce or HubSpot first?"

## What they really mean

They feel behind, and they are half hoping for a reason to wait.

## Say it out loud

Not for this, no. What you need a CRM for at the start is keeping track of people, and I can keep that in a simple list you can see. You don't need to buy a big system to find out if people want what you're selling. Once calls are coming in and someone's following up on them, a CRM makes real sense, and I'll help you pick one then. And when you do, {{data_exit}}.

## Send it by email

Subject: Do you need a CRM first?

Hi,

No, not to start. We can keep track of everyone in a simple list that you can see at any time.

The first thing to find out is whether {{their_customers}} want what you sell. Once calls are coming in and someone is following up, a CRM makes sense, and I will gladly help you choose one.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

How do you keep track of your customers and conversations today?

## When the tool is the better fit

If several people already work the same leads, or their simple list keeps losing track of deals, they should get a CRM now. HubSpot's agent is listed on its Starter, Professional and Enterprise plans, so it can come along with that choice.

HubSpot's prospecting agent page (source 5), checked 2026-11-09. Only offer a shared list if you really provide one.
