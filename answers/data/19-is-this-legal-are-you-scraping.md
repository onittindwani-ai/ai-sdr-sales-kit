# 19. Is this legal, and are you scraping LinkedIn?

**They say:** "Is this even allowed? Are you scraping LinkedIn or something?"

## What they really mean

They do not want a banned account, a complaint or a legal letter with their name on it.

## Say it out loud

Good thing to ask, because it's your name on the outreach. LinkedIn's own rules bar bots and unauthorised automation for adding contacts or sending messages, so I don't run that on your account. We use {{channels}}, we only contact {{their_customers}} where there's a real business reason, and anyone who asks to stop gets stopped. The rules differ by country, so if you're in a regulated field, your own lawyer should have the final word.

## Ask this next

Are there any rules in your field about who you can contact, and how?

## When the tool is the better fit

If they work in a heavily regulated field and a compliance team must sign off every message, an in house system that team controls is the better fit. Do not try to talk them out of that.

LinkedIn's User Agreement and help page (sources 8 and 9), read 2026-11-09. Only say this if you really do not automate LinkedIn. This is not legal advice.
