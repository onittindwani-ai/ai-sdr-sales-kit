# 16. Hunter has 240 million contacts, so how can you compete?

**They say:** "Salesforce's agent can reach 240 million contacts. How could a small agency match that?"

## What they really mean

They are impressed by the big number and assume more contacts means more customers.

I can't match that, and I won't pretend to. Apollo's announcement says Salesforce customers can reach its network of 240 million contacts through Hunter, and that's a big number. But you don't need 240 million people. You need the right {{their_customers}}, the ones who could actually buy from you, and a list that size doesn't pick them for you. ## When the tool is the better fit

If they sell to a very wide market, already use Salesforce and need reach across many regions, access to a network that size is a real advantage. For those companies, it is honest to say the agent wins on data.
