# 20. Will you feed my customer data to AI?

**They say:** "Is my customer list going into some AI model? Who else sees it?"

## What they really mean

Their customer list is valuable to them, and they do not know where AI tools send things.

## Say it out loud

I don't need your customer data to do this, and I'd rather not hold more than I need. What I need is who you want to reach and what you can honestly say to them. If you do share something, like a list so we don't email your existing customers, it's used for that and nothing else, and I'll tell you which tools touch it. And if we stop, {{data_exit}}.

## Send it by email

Subject: Where your data goes

Hi,

I do not need your customer data to run this. I need to know who you want to reach and what you can honestly say to them.

If you share a list so that we can avoid emailing your existing customers, it is used for that alone, and I will tell you which tools touch it. If we stop, {{data_exit}}.

If you need this in writing, tell me and I will add it to the agreement.

{{your_name}}

## Ask this next

Do you have any customer or contract rules on sharing data with outside companies?

## When the tool is the better fit

If their contracts or their field require that data never leaves their own systems, software inside their own environment is the better fit. An outside agency should step back unless it can work within those rules.

## Facts this answer uses

None about any vendor. List the tools you really use and only promise what you really do.
