# Sample discovery call

**SYNTHETIC.** Everyone and everything here is made up, to show how the answers and the script sound in a real conversation. It is not a transcript of a real call, and no result is implied.

**Setup:** Sam runs Example Outbound Studio (made up). Dana owns Example Cleaning Co (made up), a commercial cleaning company.

**Sam:** Thanks for making the time. I'd like to spend most of this call understanding how you get customers today. Then, if I think I can help, I'll tell you how, and if a tool or someone else would suit you better, I'll tell you that too. Does that work for you?

**Dana:** Sure. I'll be honest, I nearly cancelled. I saw that Salesforce has an AI that does all this now.

**Sam:** Fair enough, and we can get to that. Where did your last 3 customers come from?

**Dana:** All referrals. One was a building manager who moved to a new site and called me.

**Sam:** And who does the selling today?

**Dana:** Me. In the evenings, if I'm not doing payroll.

**Sam:** Do you use a CRM, like Salesforce or HubSpot?

**Dana:** I've got a spreadsheet. It's not pretty.

**Sam:** That's fine. So, on Salesforce. If you already ran Salesforce with a sales team, I'd tell you to look at it seriously. Salesforce says its agent works under rules a sales team sets, and that reps set the goal and keep full control. So it's made for a company that already has reps. You don't have that yet, and what you've told me you want is calls with office and facility managers. Who on your team would set that agent's goals and check its work every week?

**Dana:** Nobody. That would be me, in the evenings.

**Sam:** Right. Would you like to hear how I'd do it?

**Dana:** Go on. But can you promise me a certain number of calls?

**Sam:** No, and I'd be careful about promising you that without knowing how your market will respond. What I can promise is the work. A booked call means a call on your calendar with an office or facility manager at a building on your target list, and you'll see the numbers every Friday.

**Dana:** Okay. That's more honest than I expected. Send me the one pager.

**What to notice:** Sam used Answer 01 and Answer 12, asked the follow up question before explaining more, and did not say anything about what Salesforce charges or when it will launch.
