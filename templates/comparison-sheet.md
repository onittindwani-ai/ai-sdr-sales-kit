## What about Clay?

Clay's 8 October 2026 recap described a new agentic way to build in Clay and an Inbound SDR Agent, with a pre beta and a waitlist at that time (7). Inbound means it handles people who come to you, so it is a different job from outbound. If a client asks, say that, and check the waitlist status first.

## How to use this sheet

1. Recheck the status and cost rows on the day you send this. These products change often.
2. Only present the agency column if every cell is true and in your agreement.
3. If a client already has reps and a Salesforce or HubSpot setup, tell them the vendor column may suit them better.
4. Do not add claims about a vendor that are not on this sheet unless you have read them on the vendor's own page.
