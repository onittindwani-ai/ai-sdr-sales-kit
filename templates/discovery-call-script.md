# Discovery call script

A word for word script for your first call with a prospect from {{niche}}. Your job on this call is to find out whether you are the right answer, and to say so if you are not. The timings are a suggestion for a 25 minute call.

## 1. Open (2 minutes)

"Thanks for making the time. I'd like to spend most of this call understanding how you get customers today. Then, if I think I can help, I'll tell you how, and if a tool or someone else would suit you better, I'll tell you that too. Does that work for you?"

## 2. How they sell today (6 minutes)

Ask these, one at a time, and let them talk.

1. "Where did your last 3 customers come from?"
2. "Who does the selling today? How much of their week goes on it?"
3. "When someone new shows interest, what happens next, and who does it?"
4. "What have you already tried to get more of these conversations? What happened?"
5. "If I could only find you the right {{their_customers}}, how would you describe them?"

## 3. The sales team and tool check (4 minutes)

This is the part that decides whether you should sell at all.

1. "Do you use a CRM, like Salesforce or HubSpot? Who looks after it?"
2. "Does anyone on your team set sales goals or review outreach each week?"
3. "Have you looked at any AI sales tools? What did you think?"

Listen for these answers.

| What you hear | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce, reps and someone who owns it | They could run Hunter once it is open to them | Tell them so, and use Answer 01 and Answer 24 |
| HubSpot and a person with weekly time | HubSpot's agent may be enough | Tell them so, and use Answer 02 and Answer 21 |
| No CRM, no sales team, founder does the selling | This is who you serve | Carry on to section 4 |
| Founder wants to build it and has the hours | A tool may suit them better | Use Answer 04, and leave the door open |

## 4. What it would be worth (4 minutes)

1. "If {{outcome}} started next month, what would that change for you?"
2. "How many new customers could you take on right now without things breaking?"
3. "What happens if nothing changes in the next 6 months?"

Write down their exact words. Do not do the maths for them, and do not suggest a number.

## 5. Say what you would do (4 minutes)

"Here's what I'd do. {{agency_name}} runs the whole thing by {{channels}}. Before anything goes out, {{approval_step}}. Outreach starts within {{setup_time}}. A booked call means {{booked_call}}. You'd get a plain report {{report_rhythm}}. It's {{monthly_price}}, and the terms are {{contract_terms}}. And if we stop, {{data_exit}}."

Then be quiet and let them respond.

## 6. Handle what comes up (3 minutes)

Find the matching answer in this kit. Use the question under "Ask this next" before you explain anything more. If the section "When the tool is the better fit" describes them, read it and do what it says.

## 7. Close with a clear next step (2 minutes)

If it fits: "Would you like me to send over a one page summary, and we can pick a start date on a short call this week?"

If you are not sure: "What would you need to see to decide either way?"

If it does not fit: "Honestly, I don't think I'm the right answer for you, and here's what I'd do in your place."

## Rules for this call

- Never state a price for Hunter, and never say it has launched unless you have read that on Salesforce's own page today.
- Never promise a number of calls or a result.
- Never say anything bad about another company. Say what suits this client.
- Only say what is true of your business.
