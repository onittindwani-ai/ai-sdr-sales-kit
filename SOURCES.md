# Sources

Every product fact in this kit comes from the pages below. All were checked on 2026-11-09. Products, prices and availability change often. Open the page yourself on the day you use a fact.

Labels: **Primary** means the company's own page. **Vendor claim** means the company is describing itself or a partner, so say "Salesforce says" or "Apollo's announcement says" and never state it as settled fact.

## Salesforce Hunter

### 1. Salesforce news: Agentforce job ready AI agents, 11 September 2026

https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-job-ready-ai-agents/ (Primary, vendor claim)

- Describes Hunter as an outbound sales agent that works a sales pipeline from research to outreach, collaborating with sellers over weeks and months.
- Status given: "Pilot now; GA November '26." GA means general availability.

### 2. Salesforce: outbound AI sales agent page and FAQ

https://www.salesforce.com/sales/ai-sales-agent/outbound/ (Primary, vendor claim)

- The agent works "under rules a sales team sets".
- "Reps set the goal and maintain full control."
- It can "require rep approval".
- It drafts and sends outreach.
- "Hunter can perform many tasks associated with an AI SDR or BDR."
- No price was shown on this page when checked.

### 3. Apollo press release: named flagship data partner for Hunter, 15 September 2026

https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/apollo-named-flagship-data-partner-for-hunter-salesforces-new-outbound-sales-agent-302879231.html (Vendor claim, from a partner)

- Salesforce customers can access Apollo's network of 240 million contacts directly through the agent, even if they are not Apollo customers.
- "built to help sales teams grow revenue faster"
- Says Hunter was in pilot, with general availability planned for November.

### 4. Salesforce news: agentic marketing teams announcement, June 2026

https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentic-marketing-teams-announcement/ (Primary, vendor claim)

- This earlier page says "Prospecting Agent Hunter is generally available now".
- **This conflicts with sources 1 and 3**, which came later and say pilot, with general availability planned for November. We follow the later pages. On 2026-11-09 we found no page confirming the general release has happened, and no exact date. Recheck before you say anything about its status.

### 9. LinkedIn help: prohibited software and extensions

## What is opinion, not fact

These views run through the kit. They are our reading of the pages above and are labelled as opinion where they appear.

- A founder with no sales team and no Salesforce is not who Hunter is made for.
- These products are software that someone inside the company still has to set up and run.
- That gap can be a whole business for a small agency.

## Left out on purpose

- **Any price for Hunter.** No Salesforce page we read gave one. We saw packaging figures only in blogs we could not confirm, so they are not in this kit.
- **Any statistic about AI SDR results, reply rates or close rates.** We have no source we trust, and this kit has not measured any.
- **Any claim about a feature that the pages above did not state.** In the comparison sheet these are marked "Not covered".
