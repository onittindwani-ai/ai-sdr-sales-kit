# Honest limits

Please read this before you use the kit with a real client.

**This is an independent kit.** It is not made, endorsed or checked by Salesforce, HubSpot, Clay, Apollo or LinkedIn. Product names are used only to describe those products.

**These answers are not proven to win deals.** They are written to be true and usable. Nobody has measured how often they work, so this kit makes no claim about reply rates, booked calls, close rates or income. Your results depend on your offer, your market and how you deliver.

**Facts go out of date quickly.** Everything was checked on 2026-11-09, and [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) lists each page. Hunter's status is the most likely thing to change. Salesforce's own pages disagreed on it: a June 2026 page and later September pages use different wording, and we found no page confirming the general release as of that date. Recheck before you speak.

**We state no price for Hunter,** because no Salesforce page we read gave one.

**"Not covered" is not "cannot do it".** On the comparison sheet it means only that the pages we read did not say. Do not tell a client that a product lacks something unless the vendor says so.

**Some of the kit is opinion.** The idea that a founder with no sales team is not who Hunter is made for is our reading of Salesforce's wording. SOURCES.md lists these opinions.

**Every promise in an answer must be true of your business.** Some answers say that a person reads the replies, that you do not automate LinkedIn, or that the client keeps their data. If that is not how you work, change how you work or do not use that answer.

**The honest sections are there on purpose.** Each answer says when the tool is the better fit. Follow it. If you remove it, you lose what makes the answer trustworthy.

**The tool only fills in your details and checks patterns.** It does not know whether your details are true. Its audit catches missing answers, unfilled placeholders, new numbers, and certain risky claims. It can miss a false sentence that has no number in it. A person must read the final playbook.

**AI can get things wrong.** When the Claude Code skill rewrites the answers, read every one before using it.

**Examples are made up.** The agencies, prospects, prices and proof lines in `examples/` are synthetic and labelled that way. Do not copy their numbers as if they were yours.

**This is not legal advice.** Outreach rules differ by country and by industry. Check the rules that apply to you and follow every platform's terms.

**Nothing here sends anything.** The kit writes files on your own computer. It does not contact anyone, and it does not use the internet.

**What was tested.** The checker and tool were run on Python 3.9, 3.11, 3.12, 3.13 and 3.14 on a Mac. Windows and Linux were not tested by hand. The GitHub Actions workflow is set to run on Python 3.9 and 3.12 on Linux. The tool uses only the standard library.
